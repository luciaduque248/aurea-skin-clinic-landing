# AUREA Skin Clinic

> Tu piel merece contexto, no promesas.

Clinical-premium landing-page case study by Sara Duque.

**Live demo:** https://aurea-skin-clinic-landing.vercel.app

## Project
AUREA Skin Clinic is a fictional dermatology / aesthetic-medicine concept created to demonstrate ethical health marketing, conversion-focused UX/UI, responsive frontend development, accessibility, SEO and interaction quality.

The landing is designed around one primary conversion goal: **Agendar valoración**. The experience intentionally avoids fabricated medical outcomes, testimonials, credentials, urgency, pricing and social proof.

## Experience highlights
- Editorial clinical-premium art direction
- Warm ivory, deep botanical green and controlled clay palette
- Treatment-focused hero with clear primary CTA
- Interactive pre-consultation focus module without automated diagnosis
- Structured assessment flow: listen, evaluate, design and follow up
- Technology section framed around indication and clinical judgment
- Conceptual care lines for texture, tone and sensitivity
- Clinical-team integrity block with verified-data-only principle
- FAQ focused on transparency and portfolio disclosure
- Booking form with validation and local success state
- No CRM, tracking, appointment system or personal-data storage in the demo
- Responsive layout, keyboard focus and reduced-motion support
- Production metadata, canonical URL and social metadata

## Conversion funnel

## UX and accessibility
The interface prioritizes understandable health communication, visible focus states, semantic controls, responsive behavior, touch-friendly actions and reduced-motion handling. Interactive modules are framed as orientation tools, not diagnostic systems.

## Medical / portfolio integrity
AUREA is a fictional portfolio concept. It does not represent a real clinic and does not provide medical advice, appointments or treatment recommendations.

The project intentionally does **not** fabricate:
- doctors or professional credentials
- patient testimonials
- treatment success rates
- before/after outcomes
- device claims or clinical evidence
- availability, prices or appointments

In a real implementation, these elements would require verified sources and applicable regulatory review.
