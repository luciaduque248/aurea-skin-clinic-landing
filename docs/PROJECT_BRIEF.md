# Project Brief — AUREA Skin Clinic

## Purpose
This is a fictional portfolio case study built to demonstrate professional landing-page strategy, UX/UI, frontend implementation, responsive behavior, conversion thinking, interaction quality, SEO, and performance.

## Sector
Dermatology / aesthetic medicine

## Primary business goal
Convert treatment-campaign traffic into assessment bookings while building clinical trust.

## Conversion actions
- Primary: **Agendar valoración**
- Secondary: **Conocer el tratamiento**

## Visual direction
Clinical-premium rather than spa-pink: warm ivory, botanical/deep green text, controlled clay accent, editorial skin photography, evidence-oriented content blocks, calm microinteractions. Avoid beauty-salon clichés and unverifiable medical claims.

## Planned sections
- Treatment-focused hero
- Benefits and suitability
- Technology / process
- How the appointment works
- Clinical team placeholders
- Results expectations (no fabricated outcomes)
- Trust and safety
- Related treatments
- FAQ
- Booking form
- Final CTA

## Portfolio integrity
- Brand, company, people, statistics, pricing, testimonials, credentials, availability, and results are fictional unless a future source explicitly makes them real.
- Do not invent social proof or claims that could be mistaken for verified facts.
- The final case study should clearly frame the work as a concept/portfolio project.
